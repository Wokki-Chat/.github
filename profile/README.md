# Wokki Chat

**Connect with friends, Share your world & Make every conversation count**

Wokki Chat is the ultimate place to connect with friends, share your thoughts and moments freely, exciting conversations, discover new connections, and keep the people you care about just a click away. All of that without paying a dime.

🌐 **Website:** [chat.wokki20.nl](https://chat.wokki20.nl)  
📧 **Support:** support@cm.wokki20.nl

## Our Projects

### [Wokki-Chat-Python-SDK](https://github.com/Wokki-Chat/Wokki-Chat-Python-SDK)

📚 **Documentation:** [chat.wokki20.nl/developer/docs/python](https://chat.wokki20.nl/developer/docs/python/)

### [Wokki-Chat-Mobile](https://github.com/Wokki-Chat/Wokki-Chat-Mobile)
Android and iOS application for Wokki Chat (coming soon).

## Why Wokki Chat?

- **Free Forever** - No subscriptions, no hidden costs
- **Connect Freely** - Share thoughts and moments with friends
- **Stay Close** - Keep the people you care about just a click away
- **Open Platform** - Build bots and integrations with our SDK
